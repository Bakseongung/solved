```py
N=int(input())
lst=list(map(int,input().split()))
dic={}
for i,v in enumerate(lst,start=1):
	dic[v]=i
dic=dict(sorted(dic.items(), key=lambda x:x[1], reverse=True))
for i in list(dic.keys())[:3]:
	print(i)
```
